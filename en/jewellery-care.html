JEWELLERY CARE

Fine jewellery deserves thoughtful care. Discover our recommendations for preserving the beauty, brilliance and character of your FYRSTERLING jewellery.

Preserving Timeless Beauty

Every FYRSTERLING creation is designed to be cherished. With gentle handling and appropriate care, your jewellery can retain its distinctive beauty for years to come.

Whether crafted in solid 14-karat gold, rhodium-plated 925 sterling silver or adorned with lab-grown diamonds, each piece benefits from mindful everyday care.

Our guidance will help you protect your jewellery and enjoy it with confidence.

THE ART OF JEWELLERY CARE

Simple practices to preserve the beauty of your jewellery.

01 — Everyday Care

We recommend removing your jewellery during activities that may expose it to unnecessary wear or impact.

  • Remove jewellery before exercising, swimming, bathing or sleeping.
  • Avoid contact with perfume, cosmetics, lotions and household cleaning products.
  • Put on your jewellery after applying beauty products and allow them to dry first.
  • Handle delicate chains, clasps and settings with particular care.

02 — Gentle Cleaning

Regular gentle cleaning helps remove everyday residue and preserve the appearance of your jewellery.

For routine care, gently wipe your jewellery with a clean, soft, lint-free cloth.

For suitable solid gold jewellery with secure diamond settings, occasional cleaning with lukewarm water and a small amount of mild soap may be appropriate. Rinse carefully and dry completely.

Avoid abrasive materials, harsh chemicals, polishing compounds and ultrasonic cleaners unless a qualified professional has confirmed they are suitable for the particular piece.

03 — 14-Karat Gold

FYRSTERLING's yellow-gold and rose-gold jewellery is crafted from solid 14-karat gold.

Although gold is a precious and durable material, its surface may develop fine scratches through everyday wear.

Store pieces separately and avoid unnecessary friction with harder surfaces or other jewellery.

04 — Sterling Silver

FYRSTERLING's silver jewellery is crafted from 925 sterling silver with a rhodium-plated finish for additional shine.

Rhodium plating may gradually wear with use and friction. To preserve the finish, avoid abrasive polishing cloths, silver dips and aggressive cleaning products.

Gently wipe with a soft, lint-free cloth and store in a dry place.

05 — Lab-Grown Diamonds

FYRSTERLING jewellery features lab-grown diamonds, which share the same essential physical and chemical properties as mined diamonds.

Although diamonds are exceptionally hard, they can still be damaged by strong impacts, and their settings require careful handling.

Avoid exposing diamond jewellery to unnecessary knocks or pressure. If a stone appears loose, discontinue wearing the piece until it has been inspected.

06 — Storage

Proper storage is an important part of jewellery care.

  • Store jewellery separately to prevent scratches and tangling.
  • Keep pieces in a clean, soft-lined jewellery box or suitable protective pouch.
  • Store jewellery away from excessive moisture, heat and direct sunlight.
  • Fasten delicate necklaces and bracelets before storing them.

07 — Professional Attention

If your jewellery shows signs of damage, a loose setting or a weakened clasp, we recommend having it assessed before wearing it again.

You are welcome to contact FYRSTERLING to enquire about possible repair arrangements. Each request is assessed individually.
