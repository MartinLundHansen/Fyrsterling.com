9. Voluntary 14-Day Showroom Exchange Policy

FYRSTERLING offers a voluntary 14-day exchange policy for eligible jewellery purchased at our physical showroom.

Conditions for Exchange

To qualify for an exchange, all of the following conditions must be satisfied:

  • The exchange must be requested within 14 days of purchase.
  • The jewellery must be completely unused and show absolutely no signs of wear, damage or handling-related deterioration.
  • The original jewellery box must be returned in pristine condition.
  • The original FYRSTERLING bag must be included and show no signs of use or damage.
  • The original Authenticity Card must be included in pristine condition.
  • The customer must contact FYRSTERLING in advance to arrange an exchange appointment.

Unannounced returns at the showroom are not accepted under this voluntary policy.

Exchange Options

Where the conditions are met, the customer may choose between exchanging the jewellery for another FYRSTERLING jewellery item or receiving a physical paper store-credit voucher.

Store-credit vouchers are issued and handled manually by FYRSTERLING.

Cash refunds are not offered for a change of mind under this voluntary showroom exchange policy.

Exclusions

Personally engraved jewellery is excluded from the voluntary exchange policy.

This voluntary policy does not limit or replace any statutory consumer rights, including rights relating to defective goods.

10. Distance Selling & Right of Withdrawal
