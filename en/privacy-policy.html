2. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with FYRSTERLING, we may process the following information:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your email address and telephone number.
  • Information included in jewellery enquiries.
  • Details relevant to private appointments.
  • Purchase and payment-related information.
  • Delivery information, where applicable.
  • Correspondence with FYRSTERLING.
  • Information required for accounting and legal compliance.

We ask that you do not include sensitive personal information in enquiry forms or messages unless it is genuinely necessary.

3. Why We Process Personal Information

We process personal information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to customer enquiries.
  • Providing information about our jewellery.
  • Arranging and confirming private showroom appointments.
  • Preparing and fulfilling purchase agreements.
  • Coordinating payment and delivery arrangements.
  • Handling exchanges, complaints and customer service.
  • Meeting applicable accounting and legal obligations.

We do not currently operate a newsletter subscription or use customer enquiries for automated email marketing.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

The legal basis for processing depends on the purpose and circumstances.

Contractual Necessity

Under Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR, we may process personal information where necessary to perform a contract or take steps requested by you before entering into a contract.

Legitimate Interests

Under Article 6(1)(f), we may process information where necessary for our legitimate interests, including responding to general enquiries and providing customer service, provided your rights and interests do not override those interests.

Legal Obligations

Under Article 6(1)(c), we process certain information to comply with applicable legal requirements, including accounting obligations.

Consent

Where a specific processing activity requires consent, we will request it before undertaking that activity. Consent may be withdrawn in accordance with applicable law.

5. Contact Forms & Jewellery Enquiries

When you submit an enquiry through our website, the information you provide is used to respond to your request.

FYRSTERLING uses Formspree as a technical service provider for handling website form submissions.

Information submitted through these forms is transmitted to Formspree for processing and delivery to FYRSTERLING.

Formspree's handling of information is also relevant to the technical processing of your submission.

You can learn more about Formspree at Formspree Privacy Policy .

6. Private Jewellery Appointments

When you request a private jewellery consultation, we may process your name, contact information, preferred appointment details and any information you choose to share about your jewellery interests.

This information is used to arrange, confirm and prepare your appointment and to communicate with you about your visit.

7. Purchases & Customer Records

When you purchase FYRSTERLING jewellery, we may process information necessary to administer the transaction, issue purchase documentation, arrange delivery and provide after-sales assistance.

Certain transaction information must be retained to comply with applicable accounting and tax legislation.

8. Sharing Personal Information

We may share personal information with service providers where necessary to operate our business and fulfil customer requests.

This may include providers of website forms, hosting, communications, payment services or delivery services, depending on the services used for a particular transaction.

Where required, we use appropriate contractual arrangements with processors handling information on our behalf.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

9. International Data Transfers

Some service providers may process or access personal information outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where personal information is transferred to a country outside the EEA, the transfer must comply with applicable data protection requirements.

Depending on the circumstances, this may involve an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another legally recognised transfer mechanism.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

Enquiries and appointment information should be deleted or anonymised when no longer needed, unless continued retention is justified by another lawful purpose.

Accounting records are generally retained for five years from the end of the relevant financial year under Danish bookkeeping rules, subject to any applicable exceptions.

11. Cookies & Website Technologies

FYRSTERLING does not currently use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or newsletter tracking on its website.

Our website uses third-party technical services, including website hosting, Google Fonts and Formspree.

These services may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser-related data when the website is accessed or forms are submitted.

The use of cookies and similar technologies must be assessed separately. Where non-essential technologies requiring consent are introduced, the necessary information and consent mechanism must be provided.

12. Data Security

FYRSTERLING takes appropriate measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration or disclosure.

Access to personal information should be limited to those who need it for legitimate business purposes.

13. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations of applicable law, you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access your personal information.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request erasure of personal information.
  • The right to request restriction of processing.
  • The right to object to certain processing activities.
  • The right to data portability where applicable.
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise your rights, please contact info@fyrsterling.com .

We will respond in accordance with the time limits and requirements established by applicable data protection law.

14. Complaints to a Supervisory Authority

If you have concerns about how your personal information is processed, we encourage you to contact us first.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

In Denmark, the supervisory authority is the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Website: www.datatilsynet.dk
