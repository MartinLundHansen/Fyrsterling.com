FYRSTERLING GIFT CARDS

A personal gift. A timeless choice.

A GIFT OF SPECIAL SIGNIFICANCE

Some gifts are more than a simple gesture. They are a way of expressing thoughtfulness and creating memories that last.

With a gift card from FYRSTERLING, you offer someone the opportunity to choose a piece of jewellery that reflects their personal style and meaningful moments.

Our physical gift cards are presented in a beautiful dark blue case, selected with the same attention to elegance and detail as our jewellery. An exclusive presentation that makes the act of giving a special experience in itself.

GOOD TO KNOW

VALIDITY

FYRSTERLING gift cards are valid for three years from the date of issue.

REDEMPTION

Gift cards may be used as payment towards the purchase of FYRSTERLING jewellery.

PHYSICAL GIFT CARD

Our gift cards are issued in physical form and presented in our exclusive dark blue case. Digital gift cards are not currently available.

CASH REDEMPTION

Gift cards are generally not redeemable for cash. Any statutory rights to cash redemption under applicable law will, of course, be respected.
