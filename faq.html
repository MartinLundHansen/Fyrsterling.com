Explore answers to frequently asked questions about FYRSTERLING fine jewellery, diamonds, materials, personalisation and our private showroom experience.

Our Jewellery & Diamonds

What materials are used in FYRSTERLING jewellery?

FYRSTERLING fine jewellery is crafted using carefully selected precious materials. Our gold and rose gold creations are made from solid 14-karat gold, while our silver jewellery is crafted from 925 sterling silver with rhodium plating for enhanced brilliance. Our diamond jewellery features lab-grown diamonds.

Are FYRSTERLING diamonds real diamonds?

Yes. FYRSTERLING uses lab-grown diamonds, which are real diamonds with the same chemical composition, crystal structure and optical properties as natural mined diamonds. The difference lies in their origin: lab-grown diamonds are created in controlled laboratory environments rather than extracted from the earth.

What is the difference between lab-grown and natural diamonds?

Lab-grown and natural diamonds share the same essential physical and chemical properties. Natural diamonds form deep within the earth over millions of years, while lab-grown diamonds are produced using advanced technology that recreates the conditions required for diamond growth.

Is FYRSTERLING gold jewellery made from solid gold?

Yes. Our yellow gold and rose gold jewellery is crafted from solid 14-karat gold, not gold-plated silver. Solid 14-karat gold combines the beauty of precious gold with durability suited to fine jewellery.

What is the AETERNUM jewellery collection?

AETERNUM is FYRSTERLING's fine jewellery collection, inspired by the concept of eternity. The collection celebrates timeless design, meaningful connections and jewellery created to be cherished for years to come. Explore AETERNUM.

Personalisation & Jewellery Care

Can FYRSTERLING jewellery be personalised with engraving?

Selected FYRSTERLING jewellery pieces can be personalised with meaningful engravings, such as initials, dates or short messages. Engraving is subject to the design and suitability of the individual piece, and an additional fee applies. Discover personalisation .

How should I care for my gold and diamond jewellery?

We recommend handling your fine jewellery with care, storing pieces separately in a soft-lined jewellery box and avoiding unnecessary contact with chemicals, perfumes and cleaning products. Gentle, appropriate cleaning helps maintain the beauty of gold and diamonds.

How do I care for rhodium-plated sterling silver jewellery?

Rhodium-plated sterling silver should be cleaned gently with a soft, non-abrasive cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive polishing products and unnecessary friction, as these may gradually affect the rhodium finish.

Shipping, Returns & Exchanges

Does FYRSTERLING offer jewellery delivery in Denmark?

Delivery within Denmark can be arranged individually. Shipping options and charges depend on the jewellery and its value and will be communicated before the purchase is finalised.

Does FYRSTERLING offer international shipping?

International delivery may be available upon request. Please contact FYRSTERLING to discuss the destination, shipping arrangements and applicable costs.

Can I exchange jewellery purchased at the FYRSTERLING showroom?

Yes. FYRSTERLING offers a voluntary 14-day exchange policy for eligible showroom purchases. Jewellery and all original packaging must be completely unused and in pristine condition, and the original Authenticity Card must be included. Exchanges must be arranged in advance and are subject to our full terms. Personally engraved pieces are excluded from this voluntary exchange policy. Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Can I return jewellery for a refund?

For showroom purchases, FYRSTERLING does not offer cash refunds for a change of mind. Eligible items may instead be exchanged for another FYRSTERLING jewellery creation or a physical store-credit voucher. Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected. Different legal rights may apply to qualifying distance purchases.

Authenticity & Aftercare

Does FYRSTERLING jewellery come with an Authenticity Card?

Yes. FYRSTERLING jewellery is accompanied by an Authenticity Card and an individual serial number, reflecting the identity and authenticity of each creation.

Does FYRSTERLING offer jewellery repairs?

FYRSTERLING may assist with repairs of FYRSTERLING jewellery following an individual assessment. Availability, suitability and any applicable repair costs will be communicated before work is undertaken. Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Does FYRSTERLING jewellery come with a warranty?

Purchases are covered by applicable statutory consumer rights, including the Danish two-year right to complain about defects (reklamationsret). This is not an additional voluntary warranty. Please contact FYRSTERLING if you believe your jewellery has a defect.
